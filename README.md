# Project Overview

This project was conducted during an internship at the Strategy Implementation Monitoring and Analysis Unit of Vytautas Magnus University.

The objective was to benchmark annual reporting practices across 17 international universities and identify opportunities to improve KPI management, reporting transparency, data governance, and reporting automation.

The analysis was performed on publicly available university annual reports in PDF format. Since some reports contained scanned pages and image-based content, OCR techniques were applied to extract machine-readable text for further analysis.

The project combined text analytics, benchmarking, and exploratory data analysis to evaluate institutional reporting maturity and identify best practices that could be adapted for future reporting improvements.

## Analytical Approach

The analysis included:

* KPI Density Analysis – measuring the concentration of key performance indicators within reports.
* Numeric Information Density – evaluating the extent of quantitative evidence and measurable outcomes.
* Risk and Challenge Density – assessing how frequently institutions discuss risks, challenges, and improvement areas.
* Strategic Topic Analysis – identifying recurring themes and strategic priorities.
* Similarity Analysis – comparing institutions based on reporting characteristics.
* Principal Component Analysis (PCA) – identifying patterns and clusters among universities.
* Maturity Index Development – constructing a comparative indicator of reporting maturity.

## Key Outcomes

* Analyzed annual reports from 17 international universities.
* Identified reporting best practices and benchmarking opportunities.
* Developed comparative metrics and visualizations to support decision-making.
* Produced analytical findings that contributed to internal presentations for university leadership.
* Supported discussions regarding future redesign and automation of the annual reporting process, including standardized templates, improved data governance, and reporting architecture improvements.

## Repository Note

The original annual reports are not included in this repository due to their volume, storage requirements, and institutional data management considerations.

This repository focuses on the analytical methodology, code, visualizations, and findings generated during the project.
